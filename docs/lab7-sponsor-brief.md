## Prohibited Claims
Promotional drafts must not include:
- Guaranteed mortgage approval or guaranteed interest rates.
- Specific discounts, interest rates, loan amounts, or savings.
- Claims that Sunshine Mortgage is licensed, certified, or highly rated.
- Claims about business locations, experience, or customer reviews.
- Urgency language such as "Apply today before the offer expires."

- Discriminatory language or statements suggesting preferential treatment based on protected characteristics.

## Sponsor Content Correction and Unpublishing Procedure

### Responsible Person
For this classroom demonstration, the student project owner (Lupe Toledo) is responsible for reviewing, approving, correcting, and unpublishing sponsor content.
In a production application, an authorized content administrator or designated sponsor approver would perform these actions.

### Correction Procedure
1. Identify the issue: Review any reported inaccurate, misleading, discriminatory, expired, or unsupported sponsor claim.
2. Unpublish the content: Change its approval status from approved to rejected or expired, as appropriate.
3. Verify removal: Confirm that the application no longer displays the sponsor message and instead shows the neighborhood information fallback.
4. Correct the message: Prepare revised copy using only verified facts from the sponsor brief.
5. Review the correction: Check the revised message for unsupported claims, misleading wording, and compliance with the 120-character limit.
6. Approve before republishing: Record the approver and approval date. Only change the status to approved after human review.
7. Document the change: Record the original issue, correction, reviewer, date, and verification result in the AI log or sponsor review documentation.

### Emergency Unpublishing
If a sponsor message contains a potentially harmful or misleading claim, the responsible reviewer should immediately change its status to rejected.
The application must not display content unless its status is approved and it passes the expiration and length checks.

### Limitations
The current demonstration stores approval metadata in application code. It does not provide a production content-management dashboard, automated notifications, or role-based administrative permissions. Changes require an authorized developer to update and deploy the application.
