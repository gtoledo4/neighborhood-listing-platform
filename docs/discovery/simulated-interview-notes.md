# Simulated Interview Notes
## Simulated Interview: Maria Torres real estate profesional (Fictional)
### Question 1: Can you describe your current process for creating and updating property listings?
When a property is ready to be listed, I gather the basic information, such as the address, price, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, square footage, available features and amenities. I enter those details into our listing system and check them before the listing is posted. If something changes, like the price or availability, we need to make sure the information is updated everywhere it appears. Our goal is to keep everything consistent and that can take time.

### Question 2: What information do people most often need when reviewing a property listing?
Most people look for price, location, size, bedroom and bathroom counts, and the features that matter to them. Good photos and a clear description also help people understand what a property offers. If important information is missing or difficult to find, they may move on to another listing.

### Question 3: What roles or teams, if any, are involved in reviewing listing information before publication?
The roles and teams depend on the business is organized. In a small organization, the person preparing the listing might check it themselves. In a larger organization, another team member could review the details. Either way, I would want a practical way to catch mistakes before people see the listing.

### Question 4: What would make a property listing easy for people with different needs to access and understand, and how would you evaluate its success?
I would want the information to be easy to enter either on a phone or computer. Clear headings, readable text, useful descriptions of photos, and straightforward navigation would help. A successful listing would let people find the information they need without having to search through a confusing page.


## Simulated Interview: Daniel Brooks local sponsor (Fictional)
### Question 5: How do you currently share information about your business with people in the local community?
I would use a combination of a business website, social media, and local directories. Different platforms help people find different information, but it is important to keep the details consistent. If the hours or contact information change, I would want to make sure people do not find an outdated version.

### Question 6: What information about your business would you want a neighborhood listing platform to show?
I would want people to understand what the business does, location, when it is open, and how to get in touch. A short description would be useful as long as it explains the business clearly. I would also want the information to be accurate and not show services or results that the business does not provide.

### Question 7: What process, if any, would you prefer for verifying business details before they are published?
I would like to check the basic details, such as the business name, address, hours, and description. I would not necessarily need a complicated approval system, but there should be a way to report an error and request a correction.

### Question 8: What would make your business information easy for different people to access and understand, and what results would help you evaluate whether participating in the platform is worthwhile?
I would want the profile to be easy to read on a phone, with clear contact information and a description that does not use confusing language. It would be helpful to know whether people are finding the business through the platform. Customer inquiries or visits might be useful indicators, but I would want to understand what information the platform could actually measure.
