# Simulated Interview Notes
## Interview 1: Property Professional Maria Torres (Fictional)
### Question 1: Can you describe your current process for creating and updating property listings?
I collect the property address, price, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, square footage, and amenities. I also gather photographs and write a property description. After a listing is published, I update it when the price, availability, or property details change.

### Question 2: What information do people most often need when reviewing a property listing?
People usually want to know the price, location, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, square footage, and available amenities. They also want clear photographs and information about parking and accessibility. Clear and accurate details help people decide whether a property meets their needs.

### Question 3: Who reviews listing information before it is published?
The real estate professional usually prepares and checks the listing first. A broker, property owner, or other team member may also review the information. The review process helps identify missing or incorrect details before the listing is published.

### Question 4: What would make a property listing easy to use and understand, and how would you know if it was helpful?
A listing should have clear headings, simple language, readable text, and an organized layout. Photographs should include alternative text, and the website should work on mobile devices and with screen readers. Search filters should help people find properties that match their needs. Listing views, inquiries, user feedback, and viewing requests could help show whether the listing is useful.


## Interview 2: Local Sponsor Daniel Brooks (Fictional)
### Question 5: How do you currently share information about your business with people in the local community?
I share business information through social media, a business website, printed flyers, and word-of-mouth recommendations. I also share updates about business hours, services, and special offers. Keeping this information current helps people understand what the business offers.

### Question 6: What information about your business would you want a neighborhood listing platform to show?
I would want the platform to show the business name, address, contact information, operating hours, website, and a short description of the business. Photographs, accessibility information, and current offers could also be useful. The information should be clear and easy for local people to find.

### Question 7: How should business owners review and confirm their information before it appears on the platform?
Business owners should have a chance to check their business details before they are published. They should be able to report incorrect information and request updates when details change. The platform should have a clear process for reviewing and approving business information.

### Question 8: What would make your business information easy to access and understand, and how would you know if the platform was useful?
The information should use simple language, readable text, and a mobile-friendly layout. Images should include alternative text, and important information should be easy to find. Views of the business listing, website visits, customer inquiries, and user feedback could help show whether the platform is useful.
