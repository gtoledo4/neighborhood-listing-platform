# Stakeholder Discovery
Research status: Simulated stakeholder discovery no real interviews conducted.

## Stakeholder 1: Property Professional
### Known facts
1. The platform displays property listings.
2. Listing information includes details such as address, price, bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage.
3. Listing information needs to be accurate and understandable to users.
### Assumptions
- Property professionals need a straightforward way to submit and update listings.
- They want to review listing details before publication.
- They need a way to correct inaccurate or outdated information.
## Stakeholder 2: Local Sponsor
### Known facts
1. The platform includes local sponsor information associated with property listings.
2. Sponsor information and promotional claims should be accurate.
### Assumptions
1. Local sponsors want their business information displayed correctly.
2. Sponsors want to review promotional content before it is published.
3. Sponsors want to understand whether their participation benefits their business.
