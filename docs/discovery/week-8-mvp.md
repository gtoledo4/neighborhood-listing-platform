# Week 8 MVP and Work Estimates
This document estimates the size of proposed work and identifies the minimum viable product (MVP) planned for Week 8 of the Neighborhood Listing Platform project.

## Work Estimates
Work Item / Estimate / Week 8 MVP 
1. Validate property data against the property schema / small / yes 
2. Display validated property listings / medium / yes
3. Display essential property details, including price, location, bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage / small | yes
4. Display business or sponsor information / medium / no
5. Provide a workflow for requesting corrections to business information / large / no
6. Report sponsorship engagement or performance / large / no
7. Review and improve accessibility and mobile usability of core listing pages / medium / yes

## Week 8 Minimum Viable Product
The proposed Week 8 MVP will focus on a working property-listing experience.
The MVP will include:
- Property records checked against the property schema.
- Validated property listings displayed in the application.
- Essential property details visible to users.
- Core listing pages reviewed for accessibility and mobile usability.
Business-profile management, business correction workflows, and sponsorship performance reporting are deferred from this initial MVP.

## Estimation Notes
These are preliminary estimates based on the current project structure and proposed discovery requirements. Small work items are expected to involve limited changes; medium items may require coordinated changes across multiple files or components, large items may require new workflows, additional design, or more extensive implementation.
