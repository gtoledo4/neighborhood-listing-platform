# Stakeholder Personas
Simulated interview notes and AI-assisted analysis, no real stakeholder interviews conducted.

## Persona 1: Maria Torres - Real Estate 
### Background
Maria is a fictional property professional who manages residential listing information and helps prospective buyers or renters understand available properties.
- Present accurate and current property information.
- Make important listing details easy for people to find.
- Correct outdated or inaccurate information.
- Help users understand property features without navigating confusing pages.

### Potential needs and pain points
- A consistent structure for property details such as price, address, bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage.
- A straightforward way to review and correct listing information.
- Clear page layouts, readable text, and useful descriptions of images.
- A practical way to keep listing information up to date.

### Possible success measures
- Users can locate key property details easily.
- Fewer incomplete or inaccurate listings.
- Property pages are understandable and accessible across devices.

### Evidence and assumptions
The simulated responses to Questions 1–4 mention listing updates, essential property details, error prevention, and accessibility.



## Persona 2: Daniel Brooks Potential Sponsor
### Background
Daniel is a fictional small-business owner exploring how a neighborhood listing platform might present local business information.
- Present clear and accurate business information.
- Help local people understand the business's services.
- Make it easy for people to find contact information.
- Understand whether participation in the platform provides value.

### Potential needs and pain points
- A clear business profile with a name, description, location, hours, and contact information.
- A way to check business details and request corrections.
- Readable, mobile-friendly information without confusing language.
- Practical success measures that the platform can reasonably support.

### Possible success measures
- People can find and understand business information.
- Potential customers can locate contact details.
- Available engagement data helps assess whether the platform is useful.

### Evidence and assumptions
The simulated responses to Questions 5–8 mention business information, consistency, correction requests, accessibility, and possible indicators of value.

## Shared Design Considerations

The simulated material suggests several topics across both stakeholder groups:
- Accuracy and consistency of published information.
- Clear and accessible presentation.
- A manageable process for reviewing and correcting content.
- Success measures that are useful and feasible.
