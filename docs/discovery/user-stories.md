These user stories translate the simulated discovery notes and project goals into candidate requirements for my project. Because the interviews were simulated, these stories are working hypotheses.

## Property Professional Persona — Maria Torres
### US-01: View essential property information
As a person browsing property listings, I want to see the price, location, bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage on a property card, so that I can quickly compare available properties.

### US-02: Maintain accurate listing information
As a property professional, I want property information to follow a consistent structure, so that listing details are easier to review and keep accurate.

### US-03: Catch invalid property data
As a property professional, I want property data to be checked before it appears on the platform, so that missing or invalid information does not reach people browsing listings.

### US-04: Understand property photos and content
As a person browsing property listings, I want clear descriptions and meaningful text alternatives for property photos, so that I can understand important listing information using different access methods.


## Local Business Owner / Potential Sponsor Persona — Daniel Brooks
### US-05: Display essential business information
As a local business owner, I want my business profile to display my services, location, hours, and contact information,  
so that community members can understand what my business offers and how to reach me.

### US-06: Correct inaccurate business details
As a local business owner, I want a way to request corrections to my business information, so that inaccurate details can be fixed.

### US-07: Keep business information readable on mobile
As a community member viewing a local business profile, I want the information to be readable and easy to navigate on a mobile device, so that I can find business details while using different screen sizes.

### US-08: Understand sponsorship results
As a local business owner participating as a potential sponsor, I want to understand what participation results the platform can report, so that I can evaluate whether sponsoring the platform is worthwhile.
