# Content Approval Workflow
## 1. Property Listing Approval
1. Submit: A property listing is submitted or updated.
2. Validate: The listing is checked against the property data schema for required fields, valid formats, and allowed values.
3. Review: A designated reviewer checks the listing for completeness, consistency, and apparent errors.
4. Request corrections: If information is missing, invalid, or unclear, the listing is returned for correction.
5. Approve:Once the listing passes validation and review, an authorized person approves it for publication.
6. Publish:The approved listing is made visible on the platform.
7. Update: Changes to published information go through validation and review again.

## 2. Sponsor Information and Claims Approval
1. ubmit: A business provides its name, services, location, hours, contact details, and any proposed promotional claims.
2. Verify details: Basic business information is checked for completeness and consistency.
3. Review claims: Promotional claims are checked for clarity and supporting evidence. Unsupported claims are not published as verified facts.
4. Request corrections: The business is asked to correct incomplete details or provide support for claims that cannot be verified.
5. Approve: A designated reviewer approves the business information and any supported claims.
6. Publish: Approved sponsor information is displayed on the platform.
7. Correct or remove: Reported errors are reviewed, and inaccurate or unsupported information is corrected or removed.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Submitter: Provides accurate information and responds to correction requests.
- Reviewer: Checks information, records issues, and approves or rejects content according to agreed rules.
- Platform administrator: Publishes approved content and manages corrections or removals.
